Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0.7 | Last updated: August 14, 2026

Developer: LUKONICHEV DMITRII

This Privacy Policy describes how the application "Thing List" (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed by LUKONICHEV DMITRII (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer"), processes user information.

1. Collection and Use of Information

The App itself does not collect or transmit to the developer the contents of user-created lists. All lists, item names, and quantities are stored locally on the device.

2. Advertising

To maintain the free version, the App may use the Petal Ads (Huawei) and Yandex Mobile Ads advertising SDKs.

The advertising SDKs may process technical information about the device and data necessary for displaying, measuring the effectiveness of, and personalizing advertisements. Depending on the SDK used and the device settings, information related to the advertising identifier, device characteristics, ad interactions, and other technical parameters may be processed.

The data is processed by the respective advertising service providers in accordance with their privacy policies.

Huawei Privacy Policy: consumer.huawei.com/privacy

Yandex Privacy Policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential/

Yandex Mobile Ads supports GDPR requirements; for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the processing of relevant SDK data must take into account the user's consent.

3. User Rights

You may delete the App at any time using the standard method of your operating system. Upon deletion, all lists, items, and settings you have created in the App will be deleted. Data that may have been transmitted to the advertising SDKs is processed in accordance with the privacy policies of the respective providers and cannot be deleted by uninstalling the App.

You may also clear the App's data at any time through your device settings, or withdraw your consent to data processing by preventing the App from accessing your device's advertising identifier.

4. Policy Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. We recommend that you periodically review this page.
